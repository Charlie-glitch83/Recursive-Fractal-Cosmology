## Theorem

Let CIF, QV, and RFL be the role-distinct primitive sorts established by the Module A constitution, with First Action:

\[
QV(CIF)\longrightarrow RFL.
\]

Let the recursive kernel satisfy:

\[

=
\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}
\delta^{-j}e^{-\alpha jt}f_j(t),
\]

with:

\[
\delta>1,
\qquad
\alpha>0,
\qquad
\sup_{j,t}\|f_j(t)\|<\infty.
\]

Let a finite relational system have:

- a typed finite constituent set;
- directed non-self relations;
- route-local six-field witnesses;
- source-first modal certificates;
- the complete protected signature;
- the no-loss quotient;
- witnessed event charts;
- identity-preserving memory;
- split-monomorphism scale promotion;
- witness-gated specialization.

Then RFC defines an immutable triad-derived governing carrier:

\[
\boxed{

=
\operatorname{Complete}_{NB}
\left(
K_f,
\mathbf C_N,
\mathsf{Witness},
\mathsf{Event},
\mathsf{Memory},
\mathsf{NoLoss},
\mathsf{Specialize}
\right)
}
\]

for every finite \(N\) on its maximal admissible atlas.

The atlas contains:

1. a unique exact local and maximal noncollision route from every finite noncollision source state;
2. witnessed event continuations returning a unique branch, gauge family, or independent multiroute family;
3. explicit obstruction where the lawful branch set is empty;
4. exact memory and scale reopening of every protected state;
5. dormant carrier fields with zero contribution and zero backreaction until witnessed specialization.

The carrier is:

- derived from the triad and First Action;
- convergent, bounded, perturbation-stable, and conditionally differentiable on its certified kernel domain;
- locally constructive and independent of completed external trajectories;
- target-blind and no-retune;
- exact under quotient, memory, and promotion round trips;
- inherited by every applicable later RFC module.
